AI & Systems

Most of the work here automates the process you already have. I rebuild it around agents from the start: what the intake is, what a model owns, where a human signs off. Every engagement starts with a bounded diagnostic, then a scoped build you can put in front of customers. Less payroll on routing, status checks, and prep - more of it on work that moves revenue.

  • 1,008 requests in under 3 months, 91% resolved at the platform level, 20.7 hour median cycle. Requests are classified, routed to a codebase, run through an agent, then stopped at a human review gate with a rollback snapshot.
  • 0 zombie listings. The PIM, the feed, and the storefront disagreed on what was sellable. Wrote the one-way rule (a child SKU can be discontinued under a live parent, never the reverse), retired 175 SKUs, re-audited the live storefront. Zero left.
  • Search, rebuilt. One query that returned 337 suggestions now returns 16.
